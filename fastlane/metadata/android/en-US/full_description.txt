Follow football (soccer) competitions, especially Brazilian ones, in a simple, fast, ad-free app.

COMPETITIONS
• Brazilian Série A and B, Copa do Brasil, Copa do Nordeste and the Paulista, Carioca, Mineiro and Gaúcho state championships.
• Copa Libertadores, Copa Sudamericana and the Argentine league.
• Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Liga Portugal and Eredivisie.
• FIFA World Cup and MLS.
Choose which ones to follow and they show up in the menu.

FOR EACH COMPETITION
• Table with points, matches, wins, draws, losses, goals and points won. You choose the columns, and qualification and relegation zones are color-coded.
• Matches round by round, opening on the current round.
• Knockout ties with both legs, aggregate, penalties and who advanced.
• Top scorers.

MATCHES
• Today's matches from every competition you follow, with a live-only filter.
• Play by play, refreshed automatically during the match.
• Lineups on the pitch or as a list, with substitutes and substitutions.
• Comparison of possession, shots, corners, passes, fouls and cards.

FAVORITE TEAM
Mark your team to see it highlighted in tables and get a shortcut to its upcoming matches and results.

GOAL ALERTS
Tap the bell on a match to be alerted at kick-off and for every goal, with score, minute and scorer. Your favorite team's matches are added automatically. No server or Firebase involved: your phone follows the matches itself.

SIMULATOR
Fill in the scores of the remaining matches and watch the final table change instantly, with how many places each team gained or lost. Save several simulations and share yours.

WORKS OFFLINE
The latest data is kept on the device and screens open instantly, even without a connection.

PRIVACY
• No ads, no account, no tracking.
• No personal data is collected.
